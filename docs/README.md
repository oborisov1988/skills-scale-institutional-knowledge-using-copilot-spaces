# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation. This collection explains how teams take work from an initial idea through delivery and use what they learn to improve future work. Start with the [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md) for a concise introduction, then use the guides below for each stage of the lifecycle.

## How OctoAcme manages projects

OctoAcme moves work through a lightweight, iterative lifecycle:

1. **Initiate and align:** Validate the customer or business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders and owners, and agree whether the work is ready to plan.
2. **Plan:** Turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, dependencies, and a shared Definition of Done.
3. **Execute and track:** Deliver in small increments, coordinate through the project board and regular team check-ins, and use reviews, testing, metrics, and an actively maintained risk register to surface issues early.
4. **Release and deploy:** Confirm readiness, passing checks, release notes, and rollback plans; deploy and verify the change, then communicate the release to stakeholders.
5. **Reflect and improve:** Hold retrospectives after iterations, releases, or significant events. Assign owners and due dates to a small set of improvement actions and follow up on their impact.

Throughout the lifecycle, OctoAcme prioritizes **customer value**, **clear ownership**, **iterative delivery**, **evidence-informed decisions**, and **transparent communication**. Teams adapt the cadence to the work while keeping progress, decisions, risks, and responsibilities visible.
